<p>한국어에서 “말하다” 하나로 해결되는 상황이, 영어에서는 <strong>say, tell, speak, talk, mention, discuss, state, claim, announce</strong> 등 수십 가지 동사로 나뉩니다.</p>
<p>이 동사들을 잘못 선택하면 문법적으로는 맞아도 <strong>원어민에게는 어색하게</strong> 들립니다.<br>
이번 강에서는 각 동사의 핵심 차이와 자연스러운 선택 기준을 완전히 정리합니다.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>이 글을 읽고 나면:</p>
<ul>
<li>say/tell/speak/talk의 구조적 차이를 완벽히 구분할 수 있습니다.</li>
<li>mention/discuss/chat/argue/claim/state/announce를 상황에 맞게 쓸 수 있습니다.</li>
<li>같은 상황에서 어떤 동사가 더 자연스러운지 감각을 익힐 수 있습니다.</li>
<li>영어 원어민이 자주 쓰는 말하기 관련 표현 패턴을 익힐 수 있습니다.</li>
</ul>
</blockquote>

<h2 id="1-say-vs-tell--가장-기본적인-차이" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="20">1. say vs tell — 가장 기본적인 차이 <a class="header-anchor" href="#1-say-vs-tell--가장-기본적인-차이" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h2>
<p>이 두 동사는 가장 자주 혼동됩니다. 구조 차이를 먼저 이해하면 모든 것이 쉬워집니다.</p>
<h3 id="핵심-구조-차이" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="24">핵심 구조 차이 <a class="header-anchor" href="#핵심-구조-차이" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h3>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">say + (that절 / 내용)          → 사람 목적어 없음
tell + 사람 + (that절 / to부정사) → 반드시 사람 목적어 있음
</code></pre></div></code></pre>
<h3 id="say--내용-중심" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="31">say — 내용 중심 <a class="header-anchor" href="#say--내용-중심" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h3>
<p><code>say</code>는 <strong>말한 내용</strong> 자체에 초점을 맞춥니다.<br>
뒤에 사람이 바로 오지 않습니다.</p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">She said &quot;hello.&quot;
그녀가 &quot;안녕&quot;이라고 말했습니다.

He said that he was tired.
그는 피곤하다고 말했습니다.

She said she would be late.
그녀는 늦을 것이라고 말했습니다.
</code></pre></div></code></pre>
<p>사람을 언급하려면 <strong>to</strong>를 써야 합니다.</p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">She said to me that she was leaving.
그녀는 나에게 떠날 거라고 말했습니다.
(❌ She said me that...)
</code></pre></div></code></pre>
<h3 id="tell--대상-중심" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="55">tell — 대상 중심 <a class="header-anchor" href="#tell--대상-중심" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h3>
<p><code>tell</code>은 <strong>누구에게</strong> 말하는지가 중심입니다.<br>
반드시 <strong>사람 목적어</strong>가 따라옵니다.</p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">She told me &quot;hello.&quot;
그녀는 나에게 &quot;안녕&quot;이라고 말했습니다.

He told me that he was tired.
그는 나에게 피곤하다고 말했습니다.

She told him to sit down.
그녀는 그에게 앉으라고 말했습니다.
(tell + 사람 + to부정사 → 지시·명령)
</code></pre></div></code></pre>
<h3 id="고정-표현--tell-only--say-only" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="72">고정 표현 — tell only / say only <a class="header-anchor" href="#고정-표현--tell-only--say-only" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h3>
<p><strong>tell만 쓰는 표현:</strong></p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">tell the truth (사실을 말하다)
tell a lie (거짓말하다)
tell a story (이야기하다)
tell a joke (농담하다)
tell the difference (차이를 구별하다)
tell the time (시간을 말하다)
tell someone&#039;s fortune (점을 봐주다)
</code></pre></div></code></pre>
<p><strong>say만 쓰는 표현:</strong></p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">say a prayer (기도하다)
say a word (한마디 하다)
say goodbye / hello
say sorry (사과하다)
say yes / no
It goes without saying that... (말할 필요도 없이...)
</code></pre></div></code></pre>
<h3 id="비교-예문" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="95">비교 예문 <a class="header-anchor" href="#비교-예문" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h3>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">✅ He said a joke. ❌ / He told a joke. ✅

✅ She said she was sorry. ✅ / She said sorry. ✅
✅ He told me he was sorry. ✅
</code></pre></div></code></pre>

<h2 id="2-speak-vs-talk--두-번째-혼동-쌍" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="107">2. speak vs talk — 두 번째 혼동 쌍 <a class="header-anchor" href="#2-speak-vs-talk--두-번째-혼동-쌍" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h2>
<h3 id="speak--격식체-일방향적" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="109">speak — 격식체, 일방향적 <a class="header-anchor" href="#speak--격식체-일방향적" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h3>
<p><code>speak</code>는 비교적 <strong>격식체</strong>이며, 한 사람이 말하는 상황이나<br>
언어 능력을 나타낼 때 자주 쓰입니다.</p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">Can you speak English?
영어를 할 수 있나요?

The president will speak at the conference.
대통령이 컨퍼런스에서 연설할 것입니다.

Could I speak to the manager, please?
매니저와 통화할 수 있을까요?

She spoke for an hour without stopping.
그녀는 한 시간 동안 쉬지 않고 말했습니다.
</code></pre></div></code></pre>
<h3 id="talk--비격식체-쌍방향적" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="128">talk — 비격식체, 쌍방향적 <a class="header-anchor" href="#talk--비격식체-쌍방향적" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h3>
<p><code>talk</code>는 <strong>대화·상호작용</strong>에 초점을 맞춥니다.<br>
두 사람 이상이 주고받는 상황에 자연스럽습니다.</p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">We need to talk.
우리 이야기해야 해요. (심각한 대화 예고)

She was talking to her friend on the phone.
그녀는 친구와 전화로 이야기하고 있었습니다.

Let&#039;s talk about this later.
나중에 이것에 대해 이야기해요.

Stop talking and listen!
그만 말하고 들어요!
</code></pre></div></code></pre>

<h2 id="3-mention--가볍게-언급하다" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="172">3. mention — 가볍게 언급하다 <a class="header-anchor" href="#3-mention--가볍게-언급하다" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h2>
<p><code>mention</code>은 <strong>짧게, 지나가듯</strong> 언급할 때 씁니다.<br>
중요하게 다루는 것이 아니라 스치듯 말하는 뉘앙스입니다.</p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">She mentioned that she might be late.
그녀가 늦을 수도 있다고 (가볍게) 언급했어요.

Did I mention that I&#039;m getting married?
내가 결혼한다고 말했나요?

He mentioned something about a meeting.
그가 회의에 대해 뭔가 말했어요.

Now that you mention it, I remember.
그 말을 들으니, 기억이 나네요.

Don&#039;t mention it! (천만에요!)
</code></pre></div></code></pre>

<h2 id="4-discuss--깊이-논의하다" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="195">4. discuss — 깊이 논의하다 <a class="header-anchor" href="#4-discuss--깊이-논의하다" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h2>
<p><code>discuss</code>는 <strong>주제를 깊이, 여러 각도에서</strong> 다룰 때 씁니다.<br>
<code>discuss about</code>은 잘못된 표현입니다.</p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">We discussed the budget for next year.

Let&#039;s discuss this further.
이것을 더 깊이 논의합시다.

The team discussed several options.
팀이 여러 옵션을 논의했습니다.
</code></pre></div></code></pre>

<h2 id="7-간접화법에서의-say-vs-tell" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="308">7. 간접화법에서의 say vs tell <a class="header-anchor" href="#7-간접화법에서의-say-vs-tell" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h2>
<p>간접화법(Reported Speech)에서도 say와 tell의 구조 차이가 그대로 적용됩니다.</p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">직접화법: &quot;I&#039;m tired,&quot; she said.
간접화법: She said (that) she was tired. ✅
간접화법: She told me (that) she was tired. ✅

직접화법: &quot;Sit down,&quot; he said to her.
간접화법: He told her to sit down. ✅
간접화법: He said to her to sit down. ✅ (가능하지만 어색)

직접화법: &quot;Are you coming?&quot; she asked.
간접화법: She asked if I was coming. ✅
(ask는 say/tell 둘 다와 다른 구조)
</code></pre></div></code></pre>

<h2 id="8-실수하면-어색해지는-top-10" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="328">8. 실수하면 어색해지는 TOP 10 <a class="header-anchor" href="#8-실수하면-어색해지는-top-10" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h2>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">❌ She said me that she was late.
✅ She told me that she was late.
   또는 She said (to me) that she was late.

❌ Can you talk English?
✅ Can you speak English?

❌ We discussed about the issue.
✅ We discussed the issue.

❌ He told that he was happy.
✅ He said that he was happy.
   또는 He told me that he was happy.

❌ She spoke me about her problems.
✅ She talked to me about her problems.
   또는 She told me about her problems.

❌ I want to speak with you about this.
(격식체이지만 틀린 건 아님 — 단, 캐주얼하게는)
✅ I want to talk to you about this.

❌ He said me to leave.
✅ He told me to leave.
   또는 He said I should leave.

❌ Can I say to the manager?
✅ Can I speak to the manager?

❌ We need to speak about this later.
(가능하지만 딱딱함 — 일상 대화에서는)
✅ We need to talk about this later.

❌ She mentioned about her new job.
✅ She mentioned her new job.
   (mention + 목적어 직접, about 불필요)
</code></pre></div></code></pre>

<h2 id="마무리" tabindex="-1" data-src-line="441">마무리 <a class="header-anchor" href="#마무리" aria-hidden="true">#</a></h2>
<p>말하기 동사 선택의 핵심 세 가지를 기억하세요.</p>
<pre><code><div class="code-block"><div class="code-header"><span class="code-title">plain</span><button type="button" class="copy-button" aria-label="Copy code block">Copy</button></div><pre class="language-plain"><code class="hljs language-plain">① say vs tell:
  사람 목적어 있음 → tell
  사람 목적어 없음 → say
  (❌ say me / ❌ told that → 반드시 tell + 사람)

② speak vs talk:
  언어 능력·공식 발언 → speak
  일상 쌍방향 대화 → talk

③ mention vs discuss:
  가볍게 지나가며 → mention (about ❌)
  깊이 여러 각도로 → discuss (about ❌)
</code></pre></div></code></pre>
